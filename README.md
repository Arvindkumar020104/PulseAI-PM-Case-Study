# 🚀 PulseAI – AI Product Analytics Platform (End-to-End Product Management Case Study)

> An end-to-end Product Management case study demonstrating the complete product development lifecycle—from problem discovery and market research to product strategy, Agile execution, documentation, and release planning.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

PulseAI is an AI-powered Product Analytics Platform designed to help product teams monitor user behavior, analyze customer feedback, predict churn, generate AI-driven insights, and make data-driven product decisions.

This project showcases practical Product Management skills by simulating the work of a Product Manager using industry-standard tools such as Jira and Notion.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Modern product teams often struggle to:

- Understand user behavior across the product
- Identify customer pain points quickly
- Detect churn risks before users leave
- Prioritize features using data
- Track product KPIs in one place
- Convert analytics into actionable decisions

PulseAI addresses these challenges through an AI-driven analytics platform.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

PulseAI combines product analytics, AI recommendations, customer insights, and Agile execution into one unified platform.

The product enables teams to:

- Monitor product performance
- Analyze customer feedback
- Predict user churn
- Track KPIs
- Prioritize roadmap items
- Improve product decisions using AI
